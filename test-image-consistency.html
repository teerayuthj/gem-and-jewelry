✅ Consistency Checklist

  • Background gradient matches (white to light gray)
  • Border radius is consistent (12px)
  • Box shadow depth is identical
  • Subtle blue border for brand consistency
  • Image drop shadow matches for white products
  • Hover effects are synchronized
  • Transition animations are smooth and consistent
  • Color palette aligns with overall design
